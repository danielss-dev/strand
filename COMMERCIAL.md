# Commercial license

Strand is dual-licensed: [AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE) for the public distribution,
with a commercial license available as an alternative.

## Who it's for

Companies that prefer not to take on AGPL obligations, want a license their
legal team is comfortable with, or simply want to support development.

## What you get

- A one-time-purchase, perpetual commercial license for the Strand releases
  available at the time of purchase.
- The same app everyone else gets. There is no separate build — no extra
  features, no license keys, no activation, no phoning home.

## What it costs

A one-time payment per company. It's an honor system: nothing in the app
checks or enforces it. Individuals never need one — Strand is free for
personal use, forever.

## How to get one

Email <contact@danielss.dev> with your company name and you'll get an
invoice and a signed license agreement back.
