# Strand preliminary trademark search

Date: 2026-07-18

This is a documented knock-out search for the 1.0 release gate, not legal
clearance or legal advice. Trademark risk depends on confusing similarity,
goods and services, geography, priority, common-law use, and facts that these
register searches do not establish. The owner must review the results with
qualified trademark counsel before treating the `Strand` name as cleared.

## Intended use

Strand is downloadable desktop Git-client software and related hosted-review
integration. The most relevant Nice classes for the preliminary search are
class 9 (downloadable software) and class 42 (software/SaaS and technical
services). Class selection itself remains part of counsel's review.

## USPTO

The official USPTO search was run with
`CM:STRAND AND LD:true AND IC:(009 042)`. It returned 32 live records containing
`STRAND` in classes 9 or 42. The two clearest records requiring review are:

- [`STRAND`, serial 77370576](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=77370576&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)
  is an active Principal Register registration owned by Signify North America
  Corporation. Class 9 expressly covers software for controlling lighting
  systems plus related control equipment. The software field is narrower than
  a Git client, but the word mark is identical and the class overlaps.
- [`STRAND AI`, serial 99265106](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99265106&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)
  is a live class-42 application that reached Notice of Allowance on
  2026-04-14. Its services cover SaaS for tracking and attributing intellectual-
  property use in AI-generated content. The additional `AI` wording and service
  purpose differ, but it is a current software-related filing.

The USPTO itself describes an exact-word search as only a knock-out step and
says not to stop there. See
[Federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).

## EUIPO / TMview

TMview was searched for exact mark name `Strand`, then filtered to the EUIPO
office. Nine EUIPO records were returned. Material records include:

- [`STRAND`, EUIPO 000152595](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/detail/EM500000000152595)
  is registered to Signify Holding B.V. in classes 7, 9, and 11. This is the EU
  counterpart to the live lighting/control registration found in the US.
- [`STRAND`, EUIPO 017914888](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/detail/EM500000017914888)
  is registered to Strand Stainless AB in classes 6, 11, 20, 21, and 42.
- Earlier exact `STRAND` registrations covering classes 9/42 were also present
  but displayed as expired. Expired records do not establish present clearance
  or the absence of continuing rights.

TMview warns that it is not itself an official register and its displayed
information has no legal effect. EUIPO likewise says database results and
similarity reports are not exhaustive and do not determine whether a conflict
exists. See
[EUIPO search availability](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/the-office/help-centre/tm/faq-search-availability).

## WIPO

The WIPO Global Brand Database application was unavailable to the automated
browser during this pass. TMview's WIPO-office collection was therefore used
as a bounded cross-check for the exact word `Strand`; it returned four Madrid/
WIPO records. The only displayed active exact registration was in class 34
(tobacco). WIPO record 1271148 included classes 20 and 42 but was displayed as
expired. This is useful negative evidence, not a global clearance result.

WIPO says its Global Brand Database includes Madrid and participating office
collections, but also recommends searching national/regional registers and
consulting a trademark attorney. See the
[Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) and
[its FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb).

## Release decision required

Do not mark the trademark task complete or publish 1.0 solely from this search.
Before release, the owner and counsel must decide whether the live identical
class-9 registrations and related software marks permit the intended use,
whether a coexistence strategy is supportable, or whether the product should
be renamed. A full clearance search should also cover similar spellings and
pronunciations, common-law/company/product use, domains, and the actual launch
territories.
